\subsection{Nuisance Parameters Account for Imperfect Knowledge}

We would like to account for the imperfect knowledge of the detector's operation via the incorporation of said $\nparam$'s, now referred to as nuisance parameters and a are subject to further modification. That being said, our method is applicable for any other nuisance parameter a theory might have.

\subsubsection{Learning Nuisance from Control Region}

As in a real experiment, we would like to learn about uncertainties in our system from an environment that is clean of signal. For our process, we introduce two pairs of event sets, elaborated in Table \ref{table:division_of_data}.
While the signal region (SR) is a pair of two datasets that are of interest and may contain a signal, the control region (CR) is assumed to be clean of unknown physics, hence fully expressible by our known physics model and nuisance parameters.
