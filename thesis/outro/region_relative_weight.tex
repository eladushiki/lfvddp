\subsection{Discussion - Relative Weighing of the Regions}
\label{disc:region_relative_weight}

Theoretically, a hidden "hyperparameter" of our method is the relative weight with which the \gls{sr} and the \gls{cr} carry.

In practice, all of the calculations carried in this paper weighed all samples equally, while having the same average number of samples in each region. This doesn't have to be this way, and moreover - the \gls{cr} is not constrained to have any specific number of samples in a realistic scenario.

More than this is a controllable and non-restrictive "hyperparameter" of our calculation, this one can be optimized for best results in capturing signal effects while separating it from various system nuisance variations.

Although possible and even simple, this is left for future work, or any individual research that would be carried out with this method.
