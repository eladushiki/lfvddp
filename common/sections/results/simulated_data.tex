\subsection{Simulated Data}

To demonstrate effectiveness on a real example, we apply our method to simulated CMS Open Data sample \cite{CMS_OpenData_VVTo2L2Nu_2016}. We choose cuts to search for \gls{lfv}: $\dataset{A}$ is the subset of events with exactly one $\tau$ and one $\mu$ in their final states, and $\dataset{B}$ is the one with exactly one $\tau$ and one $e$. In Figure~\ref{fig:cms_open_data_train} we observe the result of a training process made with said datasets.
