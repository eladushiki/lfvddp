\section{Modelling Detector Effects}

We added a step when processing the creation of dataset, in which we have a probability distribution that might be dependent on the obsevable space. It is split to two, one part models the knowledge of the detector response, and on top of it is added an "imperfection" part, which is not reconstructed when reweighing the dataset weights, as in eq \ref{eq:def_weights}.
