\subsection{Test Statistics}

Wilks and Wald'd theorem gives us an expected behavior of the test statistic we constructed. According to Wilks~\cite{Wilks_Original} and Wald~\cite{Wald_Original}, we have that the distribution of $t$ itself should converge to a $\chi^2_d$

\begin{equation}
    t \sim \chi^2_{d}
\end{equation}

\noindent with $d$ of the number of degrees of freedom on our signal hypothesis minus those of the null hypothesis.

and so, given no new signal in SR, we should count the number of free parameters in our signal hypothesis and be able to approximate the behavior of $t$.
