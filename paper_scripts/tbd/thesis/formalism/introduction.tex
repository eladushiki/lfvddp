One particular section of the SM particles that draws attention to specific searches for new physics is the lepton sector. The SM predicts that lepton sections of the three generations - the electron, the muon and the tau -  each contain a lepton and a neutrino. In addition, foundations of the SM predict three identical generations that are broken only by Yukawa interactions, in which case we say that there is a \textbf{Lepton Flavor Universality} (LFU) in the SM. However, behaviors such as neutrino oscillations suggest that there is physics we don't yet understand.
