The results below use several sources of background and signal events.

Most background samples are generated from exponentially falling PDFs, separately in each dimension:

\begin{equation}
    \dist{\text{background}} = \prod\limits_{j=0}^k (\dfrac{1}{a} \cdot e^{a\anyevent^j})
\end{equation}

For simulated data, we use the CMS Open Data simulated \texttt{VVTo2L2Nu} sample corresponding to the 2016 collision dataset~\cite{CMS_OpenData_VVTo2L2Nu_2016}.

Signals are injected with a chosen mean number of additional events, drawn from one of the following distributions:

\begin{equation}
\label{eq:signal-shapes}
\begin{aligned}
    &\dist{\text{nd gaussian}}
    ={}\\
    &
    \frac{1}{(2\pi\sigma^2)^{k/2}}
    \exp\left[
        -\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}
        \sum_{j=1}^{k}(x^j-\mu)^2
    \right]
    \\
    &\dist{\text{multivariate gaussian}}
    ={}\\
    &
    \frac{1}
    {(2\pi)^{d/2}\sqrt{\det\boldsymbol{\Sigma}}}
    \exp\left[
        -\frac{1}{2}
        (\mathbf{x}-\boldsymbol{\mu})^{T}
        \boldsymbol{\Sigma}^{-1}
        (\mathbf{x}-\boldsymbol{\mu})
    \right]
    \\
    &\pdf_{\text{gamma3}}(\eventsymbol)
    ={}\\
    &
    \frac{s}{2}
    (s \eventsymbol)^2
    e^{-s \eventsymbol}
    =
    \frac{s^3}{2}\eventsymbol^2 e^{-s \eventsymbol},
    \qquad \eventsymbol \geq 0,
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

Before each run, the data are normalized to the range $[-1, 1]$ to ensure numerical stability of the sigmoid functions.

For generated datasets, new events are drawn in each run. For loaded datasets, background events are selected randomly from a larger sample obtained from the data source. An artificial signal is added when required.
