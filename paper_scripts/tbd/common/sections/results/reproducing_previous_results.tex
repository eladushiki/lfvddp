\label{sec:reproduction}

The implementation has undergone substantial changes, while retaining a configuration that reproduces the runs of Ref.~\cite{LastDDPPaper}. We therefore first reproduce representative results from that work. Figure~\ref{fig:t_distribution_plot_reproduction} compares the background-only distribution of $t$ with a $\chi^2$ distribution with the corresponding number of degrees of freedom. Each event has a single observable drawn from an exponentially falling probability distribution.

\begin{figure}
    \addgraph{common/sections/results/plot01-background-only-t-dist-recreation-v7/t_distribution_plot_-3734364174182558256.png}
    \caption{Training results for two equivalent datasets with no signal or detector effect. Background samples are drawn from an exponentially decaying \gls{pdf}. Each dataset contains a number of events drawn from a Poisson distribution with a mean of $1e5$.}
    \label{fig:t_distribution_plot_reproduction}
\end{figure}

With similar parameters, the distribution approximately approaches $\chi^2$, as in the earlier study. Here, the convergence is relatively stable, in contrast to the specific epoch cutoff used previously.
