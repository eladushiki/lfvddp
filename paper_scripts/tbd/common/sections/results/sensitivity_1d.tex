    \caption{Observed significances of the detection process by their injected significance. The process is run several times, with varying signal strength. The background only detection process is run 1000 times to generate a sample of the $\plratio$. Three signal shapes are picked, each is run for 100 times for each intensity from a varying set. The injected significance is calculated directly from the signal and background \gls{ndf}, as in Eq.~\ref{eq:analytic_significance_from_pdfs}. The observed significance is calculated twice; once by the percentile of the mean of the signal $\plratio$s within the background only distribution (solid line), and the number of \glspl{std} it is above the mean of the background distribution (dashed line). All $\plratio$s are calculated from datasets with identical parameters and hyper parameters as in the background only runs, apart from the added signal.}
