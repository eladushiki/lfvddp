Three signal shapes are tested, with 100 repetitions at each of several signal strengths. The injected significance is calculated from the signal and background \gls{ndf} using Eq.~\ref{eq:analytic_significance_from_pdfs}. The observed significance is evaluated from the percentile of the mean signal $\plratio$ in the background-only distribution (solid line), and from its displacement above the background mean in units of \glspl{std} (dashed line). All $\plratio$ values are calculated from datasets that, apart from the injected signal, use the same parameters and hyperparameters as the background-only runs.}
