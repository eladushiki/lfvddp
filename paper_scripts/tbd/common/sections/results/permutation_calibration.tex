\paragraph{In the Lack of Pure Background}

For an experimental sample that may contain a signal, a background-only toy sample is not available. Following Ref.~\cite{LastDDPPaper}, we estimate the background-only behavior through permutation tests: the $\dataset{A}$ and $\dataset{B}$ samples are shuffled and the test statistic is recalculated. Repetition yields a distribution indistinguishable from the background-only distribution, as shown in Figure~\ref{fig:permutation_test}.
